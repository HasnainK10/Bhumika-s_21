A little birthday note.

Happy 21st, Bhumika. 🤍

I wanted to wish you in a way that felt a little more personal than just sending you a birthday text. We've only recently started talking again, but somehow you've already given me a lot of moments worth remembering.

From that café, the drive, our walk by the beach and momos, to all the random reels, late night conversations, sunset plans and mountains we've somehow added to our imaginary map I'm genuinely glad I got to meet you again.

And somewhere along the way, I also realised just how ridiculously cute you are. 😌 Whether you're sending me some random reel, getting excited about a place you want to visit, or getting annoyed over the smallest things, you somehow manage to be cute without even trying. And I don't know if I've told you this enough, but you genuinely sound adorable when you're angry. 😂 So yes, your little temper is something I've secretly grown quite fond of.

And since you once said “no one's Kanmani yet”... I think you might be forgetting something. You're already the Kanmani of our eyes. 😉🤍

I hope 21 brings you everything you've been wishing for. I hope you get to travel to all those beautiful places you've shown me, watch countless sunsets, go on spontaneous drives, laugh a lot, and make the kind of memories that you'll randomly smile about years later.

And please, take care of yourself too. Take your meds and supplements on time, get some proper rest, and don't be too stubborn about looking after yourself. I know you have a lot going on sometimes, but you deserve to take care of yourself just as much as you take care of everyone else.

And one more thing please don't overthink things or feel like you have to keep everything to yourself. If something's bothering you, if you want to rant, if you're having a bad day, or if you simply feel like talking at 2 AM about absolutely nothing, you can always talk to me. I'll always be there to listen, so you never have to hesitate or wonder whether you're bothering me. Just talk to me, okay? 🤍

I really hope your dad feels better soon and that you get to celebrate many, many more birthdays with him. I genuinely hope the coming year brings your family more peace, happiness and reasons to smile together. 🤍

And selfishly, I hope this year gives us a lot more of those random plans we've talked about more drives, more sunsets, more places to mark on that map, more ice creams, and probably way too many reels being sent back and forth. 😂

Here's to 21.
I hope this year is as beautiful, chaotic, cute and unforgettable as you are.

Happy birthday, cutie. 😌❤️
